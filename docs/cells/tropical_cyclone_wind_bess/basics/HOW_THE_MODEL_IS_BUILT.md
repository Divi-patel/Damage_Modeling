# Why this proposal has this form

**Question:** price a first BESS wind response without pretending a generic
Substation curve is battery evidence.

**Evidence:** OEM documents establish relevant mechanical hazards and distinct
anchorage configurations; a project drawing identifies its site design basis.
Neither provides a target repair curve. Existing Solar electrical responses
provide a reusable *synthetic* normalized-demand probability skeleton. The
original PVSCM inputs provide related cost-group proportions.

**Grain:** keep integrated cabinets and separate PCS. Preserve source cost
bundles; do not manufacture independent module/rack curves or count internals
again after cabinet replacement. EBOS/civil/fieldwork remain unbound.

**Axis:** comparable pressure-demand/reference ratio. A squared gust ratio
requires a named compatible wind bridge; a bare 66 m/s product label cannot
fill it. The national reference remains unselected at this checkpoint.

**Form:** ordered states preserve separate assumed probabilities and economic
consequences. Borrow the electrical T4 skeleton explicitly; recost enclosure
states to the same cabinet value. Retain unweighted resistance scenarios and a
separate repair-policy sensitivity. Neither is a confidence interval.

**Decision:** the owner selected full replacement at major compromise for local
testing, with repair retained as a sensitivity. That choice is conservative
about serviceability, not a measured mean or a bound on total real risk.

**Emit:** conditional mean unit DR and same-unit cost after exact pins, local
opt-in and explicit hypothesis/scenario. No intrinsic uncertainty distribution
or plant distribution is supplied by the local reader.

**Ship:** [v1.0 current package](../current/README.md), conditionally admitted
after local source/reference and annual-cap/distribution qualification. National
support and recipient delivery remain separate. Dossier, exact sources and future
triggers: [derivation](../proposed/local_candidate_v0_1/derivation.md).
