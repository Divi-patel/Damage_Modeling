## Site and exposure adapter

This local package specifies demand fields; it does not qualify national source
transfer. Demand must already be a declared comparable pressure/reference ratio.
The squared-gust bridge is conditional on matching averaging, height and exposure.
No mounting-height discount, wall/fence shielding, storm-kit credit, suppression
credit or favorable direction/duration default is used. Unknown source demand
withholds response; absence of mitigation evidence earns no credit.

Do not apply a site correction both in the delivered demand and again to DR.
Do not multiply an uncertain affected-value share into a curve that already
defines mean response of that unit. Do not charge internal cooling/BMS/cells
again after integrated replacement. Shared support/logistics remain unbound
instead of being damaged independently or silently included twice.

The target remains one selected outdoor liquid-cooled LFP design. No construction
rating, field survival or suppression test establishes its probability model.
The shape, target transfer, repair policy, wind-reference bridge and value
allocation limitations all remain visible in capability and output flags.
