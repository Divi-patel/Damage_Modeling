## Immediate work boundary

The $84M enclosure group dominates the response research. Work on the
ingress-to-replacement chain and the $10.4M PCS candidate first. Retrieve only
evidence that changes the candidate: vulnerable-entry geometry, water-damaged
repair unit, compatible construction, or included costs. No cell-level battery
simulation, general shared-runtime registry or ground-up flood model is needed.

If published calibration is unavailable, expose the minimum engineering
assumptions required by a screening candidate and test their consequences. Do
not hide assumptions in an allegedly empirical curve. If that cannot support a
requested output, retain the exact unsupported pair and continue qualified work.
Do not treat the whole project as blocked merely because refinement remains.
